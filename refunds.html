Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Plain version: cancelling takes four taps in the Play Store, it never needs a message to us, and nothing you have already made is taken away from you when you stop paying.

Cancelling

A Tradeslip subscription is bought and held by Google Play, not by us. You cancel it yourself, at any time, without contacting anybody:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Tap Subscriptions, then Tradeslip.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription.

You can also cancel from a computer at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep the paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for.

We cannot cancel it for you, and we never ask you to. No app in this category should make you write to support to stop paying. If you ever find a screen in Tradeslip that seems to require that, it is a bug - please report it.

What happens to your work if you stop paying

  • Every estimate, invoice, client and price-book entry stays on your phone, readable and editable, exactly as before.
  • Every PDF you have already created is a normal file on your device. It is yours. Nothing expires it.
  • Export never stops working. The full JSON backup and the CSV export in Settings are available whether or not you are subscribed, and always will be. Your records are not held to ransom by a bill.
  • What ends is the ability to send new documents as a PDF beyond the free allowance. Subscribing again restores it immediately; nothing is lost in between.

Refunds

Because the purchase is made through Google Play, Google processes refunds and Google's refund policy applies. Two routes:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase you can usually request a refund directly in the Play Store, and it is handled automatically.
  • After that, request it through Google Play or ask us. We support any reasonable refund request a user raises with us - including a renewal you did not intend, or a subscription you did not use. We will not argue about a renewal you clearly forgot.

This does not affect any refund right you have under the law where you live, which is set out below.

Your statutory right of withdrawal (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are a consumer in the European Union, the EEA or the United Kingdom, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract for digital content, without giving a reason.

A subscription unlocks the paid features straight away. By confirming the purchase you ask for that performance to begin immediately during the 14-day period, and you acknowledge that once it has been fully performed you lose the right of withdrawal for that period - as provided by Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU and, in the UK, regulation 37 of the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

Within the 14 days, and to the extent performance is not yet complete, you may withdraw by cancelling in Google Play and telling us through the support page. You do not need to use a particular form of words; saying you wish to withdraw is enough.

Automatic renewal

  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until cancelled.
  • The price, the currency and the renewal period are shown in the app, and again by Google Play, before you confirm - never after.
  • Google Play emails you a receipt for every charge, including renewals.
  • Cancelling is available at all times through the steps above, with no fee and no conversation.
  • If the price of an existing subscription ever changes, Google Play notifies you in advance and the new price does not take effect until you agree to it.

Unauthorised or duplicate charges

If you see a charge you do not recognise, report it to Google Play first - they hold the payment record and can reverse it. Tell us as well through the support page so we can check whether anything on our side caused it.
