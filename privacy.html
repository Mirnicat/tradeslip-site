Privacy policy

The short version: Tradeslip keeps your work on your phone. We do not have a server, and we could not read your estimates even if we wanted to.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who is responsible

Tradeslip is developed and published by Mirnicat Seyidzade, an individual developer based in Azerbaijan, under the name Mirnijat Studio. Contact: the support page.

What stays on your device and never leaves it

All of the following is written to private storage inside the app and is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party:

  • Your business details, logo, default terms, tax rate and numbering
  • Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, site addresses and notes
  • Estimates, line items, prices, your internal cost and margin figures
  • Client signatures captured on screen
  • The PDF documents the app generates

The app declares no Android permissions. It cannot read your gallery, your contacts, your location, your call log or your SMS. When you attach a logo, the Android system photo picker hands the app that one image only.

When the app talks to the internet, and what it sends

Tradeslip makes no network connection of any kind until you open the subscription screen. Browse, price, sign and export as much as you like: nothing is contacted and nothing is sent.

The first time you open the subscription screen, the billing component starts. Purchases themselves are processed by Google Play Billing, and subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which acts as our processor. From that point the following leaves the device:

DataWhy
A randomly generated anonymous app user IDTo tie a purchase to an installation so your subscription can be restored
The Google Play purchase receipt / token, product ID, purchase and expiry datesTo verify the purchase is genuine and to decide whether the subscription is active
Basic device and store metadata (platform, app version, country, store)To process the receipt against the correct store

We do not send your name, email address, payment card details, client data or any estimate content. We never receive your card number - Google handles payment. This data is used only to run the subscription. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Once you have opened the subscription screen, the app remembers that locally and will check your subscription status on later launches, so a subscription you already paid for is recognised without you having to restore it by hand.

Ratings and links to Google Play

Once you have used the app for a while, Tradeslip may ask Google Play to show its own rating card - only right after an invoice has been paid in full, and at most a few times in all. The card belongs to Google Play and runs under Google's terms. The app is not told whether you rated it or what you wrote, and nothing from your documents, clients or prices is passed on. When the app last asked is kept in a small file on the phone and is not included in backups.

"Rate Tradeslip", "Tell a colleague" and "Manage subscription" in Settings simply open Google Play or your phone's share sheet. The app itself sends nothing when you tap them.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No advertising SDK and no advertising ID. The app removes the AD_ID permission outright.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting SDK.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Sharing a PDF

When you tap send, Android opens its own share sheet and you choose the app. From that moment the document is handled by whichever app you picked and by that company under its own privacy policy. Tradeslip never sends anything by itself.

Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so your client data is not copied into a Google backup.

Children

Tradeslip is a tool for working tradespeople and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Because the data lives on your device, you are in direct control of it - see deleting your data. For the subscription records held by our processor, you may request access or erasure by writing to us through the support page. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our lawful basis for processing subscription data is performance of the contract between you and us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the updated version is published on this page with a new date, and material changes are noted in the app release notes.
