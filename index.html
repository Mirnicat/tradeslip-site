Tradeslip

Price the job on site, get it signed on the phone, send the PDF before you leave the driveway.

For plumbers, electricians, carpenters and small repair crews.

Offline first

Every calculation, the whole database and the PDF renderer run on your handset. There is no account to create, no server to depend on and no signal required. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere.

What it does

  • Tap-to-add price book so a quote is a handful of taps, not a form
  • Labour, materials, equipment, waste allowance, discount and tax handled properly
  • Your own margin shown while you price - and only to you, never on the client copy
  • A print-quality A4 PDF with your logo, your terms and your numbering
  • Client signature captured on the screen and embedded in the document
  • One tap to the share sheet: WhatsApp, Telegram, email, anything installed
  • Polite payment reminders, and follow-ups for estimates the client has not answered
  • An income report by month - invoiced, received and tax - ready for your accountant

Nothing is ever lost

Take a call mid-quote, get a low-memory kill, or simply put the phone in your pocket - every keystroke is already committed to local storage. Drafts do not evaporate.

Pricing

Free to install and free to use. The first three documents you send as a PDF cost nothing. After that, unlimited sending is one subscription billed through Google Play - monthly or yearly, one price per phone, no per-user fees. Your price is locked while you stay subscribed. Re-sending an estimate you have already delivered is always free, so a job in progress never hits a wall.
