Common questions

I lost my phone. Can you restore my estimates?

No, and that is by design - your estimates were never on our servers, so we have no copy. Keep the PDFs you send; they are the durable record.

I paid but the app still shows the free tier

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to the Google account that bought them, so make sure the phone is signed in to that account.

How do I cancel?

Play Store › profile picture › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions › Tradeslip › Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Can I change the currency or the tax rate?

Yes - business details screen. The currency symbol, default tax rate, default waste percentage and the estimate number prefix are all yours to set.

Why does the app ask for no permissions?

Because it does not need any. Adding a logo uses the Android system photo picker, which hands over the single image you choose without granting gallery access.

Something is wrong with a number on my PDF

Tell us the line, the quantity, the rate and what you expected. Money is calculated in whole cents throughout, so a discrepancy is a bug worth fixing and we will treat it as one.
