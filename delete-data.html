Deleting your data

Tradeslip stores your data on your device, so deleting it is something you do directly and it is immediate.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

App: Tradeslip (com.mirnicat.tradeslip)
Developer: Mirnijat Studio - Mirnicat Seyidzade, Azerbaijan
Contact: tradeslip.support@gmail.com

1. Delete one estimate

Open the estimate list, press and hold the estimate, confirm Delete. The estimate and all of its line items are removed immediately and permanently.

2. Delete a client

Open the client list from any estimate and remove the client there. Estimates you already created keep their history but are no longer linked to that client.

3. Delete everything on the device

Android Settings › Apps › Tradeslip › Storage › Clear storage (some phones call it "Clear data"). This erases the database, your business profile, your logo, every saved signature and every generated PDF. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

There is no cloud copy and no backup on our side, so once you have done this the data is gone. It cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else.

4. Delete subscription records

If you subscribed, a record of the purchase exists outside your phone, held by Google Play and by our billing processor RevenueCat, Inc. It contains an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt - not your name, email or card details.

To have that record erased, email tradeslip.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. Include the Google account email used for the purchase, or the order ID from your Play Store receipt, so we can locate the record.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataOutcomeTiming
Estimates, clients, prices, signatures, PDFs, business profile Deleted by you on the device; we never held a copyImmediate
Anonymous app user ID and subscription receipt Deleted on request Request acknowledged within 7 days, deleted within 30 days

Deleting the subscription record ends the ability to restore that subscription on a new device. Cancelling a subscription is separate and is done in the Play Store.
