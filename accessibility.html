Accessibility statement

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Tradeslip is used one-handed, in bad light, on a phone screen, often by someone with cold or dirty hands standing in an unfinished bathroom. Designing for that and designing for accessibility turn out to be mostly the same job.

What is supported

  • Screen readers. Every control has a text label or tooltip that TalkBack reads. Icon-only buttons are labelled; decorative graphics are not announced.
  • Larger text. The app follows the system font-size setting and its layouts reflow rather than clip. Very large settings are capped at 160% so that the number keypad and the totals stay on screen together - beyond that, rows would start hiding each other.
  • Touch targets. Interactive controls are at least 48 dp, which is the Android minimum, and the main actions are considerably larger.
  • Contrast. Text and interface colours are chosen to meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text) in both light and dark themes.
  • Dark mode. Follows the system setting, or can be forced to light or dark in Settings.
  • No colour-only meaning. Status, margin warnings and paid/unpaid are always carried by text or an icon as well as by colour.
  • No time limits. Nothing in the app expires while you are typing, and every keystroke is saved within a fraction of a second, so an interruption never costs you work.
  • No motion required. There are no gesture-only actions: everything reachable by a swipe is also reachable from a button or a menu.
  • Generated PDFs use real, selectable text rather than an image of text, so a client using a screen reader can read the estimate you send them.

Known limitations

Being honest about these is more useful than claiming perfection:

  • The signature pad requires drawing on the screen. There is no non-drawing alternative inside the app; a document can always be sent unsigned and signed by other means.
  • At the largest system font sizes some table rows in the on-screen totals wrap onto two lines.
  • The app has not been independently audited. The conformance described here is our own assessment against WCAG 2.2 level AA as it applies to a native mobile app.

Feedback

If something in Tradeslip is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us through the support page and describe what happened and what assistive technology you use. Accessibility reports are treated as bugs, not as feature requests, and we will reply within 5 working days.
